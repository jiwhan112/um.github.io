# EXP-2D-001 · 승인한 프롬프트 v1.0

> 이 문서는 실행 이전에 승인된 **설계 프롬프트**입니다. 내부 이미지 생성 도구에 실제 전달된 비공개 payload와 동일하다고 주장하지 않습니다. 실제 출력은 2회 모두 4종 합성 이미지였습니다.

```text
Create exactly ONE isolated 2D fantasy RPG inventory icon of a potion bottle.

ART STYLE:
Polished stylized cartoon game art, readable silhouette, clean smooth edges, consistent medium-weight dark navy outlines, simplified cel shading with 2-3 tonal levels, high readability at small UI sizes.

FIXED BOTTLE DESIGN:
A compact rounded-square glass bottle, short narrow neck, natural cork stopper, thin bronze collar, front three-quarter view with a small amount of the right side visible. Centered upright composition. The bottle should occupy approximately 80% of the canvas height, with clear padding on all sides.

LIGHTING:
Soft upper-left light, consistent highlights on the glass, restrained reflections, consistent material appearance.

POTION DETAILS:
{POTION_SPEC}

OUTPUT:
One complete icon on a square canvas, genuinely transparent background with clean alpha edges. No floor, cast shadow, environment, decorative scene, UI border, typography, labels, watermark, extra objects, or additional bottles.

The bottle geometry, cork, outline treatment, lighting direction and detail density must remain consistent across the entire four-icon asset family.
```
